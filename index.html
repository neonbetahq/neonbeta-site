About NeonBeta

NeonBeta helps gamers find game deals worth buying. Sales are noisy, so we focus on three things: real historical lows, free games you can claim, and fresh releases.

How it works

We collect prices from free tools like CheapShark and from store sales, then show them in USD or EUR. Each deal gets a short verdict, such as whether it is the lowest price on record, so you can tell a real bargain from an ordinary discount. Prices change fast, so always check the store before you buy.

How we make money

Some links on NeonBeta are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It keeps the site and the Discord running. We aim to list deals based on value, not on commission.

Made by gamers, for gamers

NeonBeta is a small, independent project that we make for fun. It is made by gamers who like a good deal, and it is still growing, so things will change and improve. Tell us what is missing in our Discord.
